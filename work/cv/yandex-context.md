## Role

- The app is massive, so he always worked as part of a team. He did not
  build the listed features from scratch; he contributed to them to
  different degrees, "only a few parts".
- Most of his work was related to the map: he worked a lot with the internal
  map APIs for all these features and learned a lot about how the map works
  (Yandex Maps, internationally Yango Maps).
- Also worked on the customer app, not in depth: helped on a few features.

## Flutter

- Flutter was just starting out. This was his very early Flutter exposure:
  he was among the first at Yandex to use it, which later carried over to
  talabat.

## Uber code

- Around the time he joined, Yandex had taken over the Russian part of
  Uber's business and inherited a lot of its code, including the RIBs
  architecture. He was exposed to a lot of core Uber code and learned from
  that codebase.
