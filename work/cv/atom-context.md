# Atom: context from Art (for picking CV content, not CV text)

Told by Art on 2026-11-24, on top of the LinkedIn text.

## What it was

- Atom was a startup. It was Art's first exposure to Android development
  and his first commercial Android project.
- First app (Barev): a dating app for an Armenian audience. A full-fledged
  app with chat, a feed, connection requests and a lot of pictures, similar
  to Badoo but with a simpler UI. Written in Java.
- He built it from the start and launched it. Worth saying in the CV.
- He is not proud of how it is built internally; he had little experience
  then (private context; not for the CV).
- Second app: designed and implemented but never released. Art thinks the
  owner decided to invest in something else. It was better built, with a
  better architecture. Written in Kotlin.

## What he took from it

- Learned a lot, and was hired by MTS afterwards on the strength of it.
- Saw how startups work and how decisions are made. Took part in some of
  the decisions, though not to the biggest degree.
- Worked on the back end a bit, since in a startup everyone does
  everything. Learned some Django, which helped in many later projects.
