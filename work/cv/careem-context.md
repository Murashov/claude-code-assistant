- Hired to **lead the team responsible for the integration with Hala**
  (the Hala team).

- **Hala:** Dubai's city taxi.
- The integration goes through an intermediary company, **Trapeze**, which
  provides the hardware and software of the **taximeter** that the Careem
  app connects to.
- The taximeter is approved by the **RTA**, Dubai's Roads and Transport
  Authority (Art said "Road Traffic Authority").
- The team built features that depend on the Trapeze integration and on
  alignment with the RTA and Hala.

## The real challenge: alignment, not engineering

  boolean change in the protobuf.
