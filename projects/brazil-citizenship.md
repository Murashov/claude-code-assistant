## Goal

## Context

- Art lives in Curitiba, Brazil. His daughter was born in Brazil around January 2026,
  and Art holds residency on that basis.
- The route is ordinary naturalization. With a Brazilian child the
  residence period is 1 year of indefinite residence (confirmed on the MJ
  page, 2026-11-26). Full research and document checklist:
  [docs/citizenship/naturalization-research.md](../docs/citizenship/naturalization-research.md).
- Main blockers: proof of Portuguese (Celpe-Bras, or a passed Portuguese
  course for immigrants at an MEC-accredited university) and the Russian

- Tax regularity in Brazil is tracked separately in [brazil-taxes](brazil-taxes.md).
