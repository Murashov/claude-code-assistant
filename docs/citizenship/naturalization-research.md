## 1. Who can apply and when

Source: Ministério da Justiça, naturalização ordinária page
https://www.gov.br/mj/pt-br/assuntos/seus-direitos/migracoes/naturalizacao/o-que-e-naturalizacao/naturalizacao-ordinaria

- Age 18 or older.
- Residence **por prazo indeterminado** (indefinite), registered by the
  Polícia Federal. The clock starts on the date indefinite residence began.
- Standard period: 4 years. **Reduced to 1 year with a Brazilian child**
  (born or naturalized). This confirms the earlier "to verify" note.
- Absences: the page gives limits per case (12 months for the 4-year
  route; for the reduced routes the summary showed 3 months). To verify
  exactly which limit applies to the 1-year route before filing.
- Able to communicate in Portuguese (proof list in section 3).
- No criminal conviction, or rehabilitated.

Filing: free, online through the "Naturalizar-se" form, then an in-person
Polícia Federal appointment with originals and biometrics. Estimated up to
180 days. The decision is published in the Diário Oficial da União. Appeal
within 10 days if denied. After approval: collect a new CRNM at the PF and
register to vote within 1 year.
Source: https://www.gov.br/pt-br/servicos/solicitar-naturalizacao

## 3. Proof of Portuguese: Celpe-Bras is not the only option

Accepted (MJ PDF item 13, Portaria 623/2020 art. 5):

- Celpe-Bras certificate (Inep).
- Degree or postgraduate degree from a Brazilian MEC-accredited institution.
- OAB exam.
- **Completion, with satisfactory result, of a Portuguese course for
  immigrants at an MEC-accredited higher education institution.** The MJ
  page adds: "Só matrícula não vale": the statement must say he passed.
- ENCCEJA (primary or secondary school equivalency exam).
- Brazilian school records, public university appointment, Revalida.

Why it matters: the immigrant course route could be faster than waiting for
Celpe-Bras 2027/1 (registration about Feb 2027, results months later).

Options in Curitiba (to verify that each certificate is accepted):
- Celin-UFPR "Português para estrangeiros" (PLA) courses, including
  distance learning (PLA-EAD). UFPR is a federal MEC-accredited university.
  https://celin.ufpr.br/exame-celpe-bras-3/ (links to the PLA section)
- PBMIH (Português Brasileiro para Migração Humanitária), a free UFPR
  extension project for migrants in Curitiba since 2013. Aimed at
  humanitarian migrants; whether Art qualifies is to verify.

Celpe-Bras in Curitiba:
- Test centre: Centro de Línguas (Celin), UFPR, posto aplicador since 1999.
  Fee R$259 (page updated 30 Sep 2024; to verify for 2027). Payment by PIX
  or GRU, then send the receipt to the centre. Contact: celpebras@ufpr.br.
  https://celin.ufpr.br/exame-celpe-bras-3/
- Older Inep listing gives the address Rua XV de Novembro 1441, Centro,
  phone +55 41 3363-3354 (old, to verify).
  https://download.inep.gov.br/outras_acoes/celpe_bras/postos_aplicadores/2016/postos_aplicadores_CelpeBras.pdf
- Other Paraná centres: UEL (Londrina), UNILA (Foz do Iguaçu).
  https://portal.unila.edu.br/proint/nucli/exames-de-proficiencia
- Capacity per centre: published in the Celpe-Bras system with each edital
  (Edital Inep nº 92/2026 for 2026/2). Curitiba capacity: not found.
- Minimum level for naturalization: the MJ pages list "Celpe-Bras
  certificate" without a level. To verify whether the lowest certified

## 4. Russian criminal record certificate (справка о несудимости)

Source: ppt.ru article of 2024-10-10
https://ppt.ru/art/sudimost/spravka-ob-otsutstvii-sudimosti-s-apostilem-kak-i-gde-poluchit
and mos.ru https://www.mos.ru/otvet-dokumenti/kak-poluchit-spravku-ob-otsutstvii-sudimosti/

- Request from the MVD information centre via Gosuslugi or an MFC. Free.
  Up to 30 days (can be extended by 30).
- Apostille: only on the **paper original**, placed by the MVD. Fee
  2,500 RUB per document (2024; to verify current). Up to 5 working days,
  up to 30 if signature samples are missing.
- The electronic Gosuslugi PDF cannot get a paper apostille. Whether
  Brazil accepts an electronic apostille: to verify.
- From abroad: either a representative in Russia with a notarized power
  of attorney, or the Russian consulate (consulate route described by one
  source only; to verify with the Russian consulate serving Paraná).
- Total time: about 37 days without delays, over 100 in the worst case.
- Brazil and Russia are both in the Apostille Convention (Brazil since
  2016, Decreto 8.660/2016, cited in the MJ list), so no consular
  legalization is needed.
- Validity: the MJ page says an expired foreign certificate can be
  replaced with a declaration, but how old the certificate may be at
  filing is to verify. Plan to request it close to filing.

UAE / Dubai certificate: per the MJ page, only the country of origin is
required, so the Dubai police certificate is probably not needed. To verify
(for example by email to dinat@mj.gov.br) before skipping it.

## 6. Order of steps (draft)

1. Confirm the residence start date and that the CRNM says "prazo
   indeterminado" (question for Art). Eligibility = start + 1 year.
2. Choose the Portuguese route (Celpe-Bras 2027/1 or a UFPR immigrant
   course). This is the longest item.
3. About 2-3 months before filing: Russian certificate with apostille via
   a representative or the consulate.
4. Sworn translation in Curitiba.
5. Brazilian certificates (TRF4, TJPR), CPF status, proof of residence:
   online, right before filing.
6. File on Naturalizar-se, then the PF appointment.
